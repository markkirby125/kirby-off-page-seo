# Module 16: Entity Corroboration & Force-Indexing

*Source: James Dooley ("Money-Hat SEO") & TJ Robertson / The Edward Show, Episodes 1116 & 1134*

Before syndicating claims, finish on-page pruning in `kirby-aiseo-skill` Module 2 §2.34 so weak URLs are not corroborated.

To dominate Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and AI Overviews, you must actively "clarify" your entity across the highest-trust nodes on the web. AI search engines map the world via semantic triples (Subject → Predicate → Object) and look for multi-domain consensus. A single claim on a brand website is often ignored.

### **16.1 High-Trust Node Clarification**
*   **Target Nodes:** AI engines heavily weight canonical databases. You must inject your entity into Wikidata, Crunchbase, IMDb (if applicable), and industry-specific registries. SaaS **launch** directories (Product Hunt, BetaList, gap audits) are Module 18 §18.13 — this section remains canonical entity parity, not directory link-building.
*   **Data Parity:** Ensure the data across these nodes is mathematically identical (same address format, same executive names, same founding dates). Discrepancies cause LLM hallucination and citation dropping.

### **16.2 Cross-Platform Corroboration (Force-Indexing)**
*   **The Consensus Playbook:** Take a single piece of high-value social proof (e.g., a major client testimonial or an industry award).
*   **Syndication:** Syndicate that *exact* claim across Reddit, LinkedIn, Twitter, and niche podcasts.
*   **The Result:** Because the claim is identical and hosted on multiple DA 90+ domains, it forces AI search engines to index it as a corroborated, objective fact. This dramatically increases the likelihood it gets pulled into the AI Overview snippet as a trusted citation.

### **16.5 The Multi-Node Anchor Framework & Entity Juice (V6-D)**

*Source: James Dooley / Charles Floate — "Entity SEO for AI Search" (V6), 2026. Single-source, directional.*

* **Entity Juice (metric reframing):** replaces link-juice thinking for entity SEO — an entity's strength is its **confidence** (verified nodes), **relationship clarity** (unambiguous triples), and **proximity to trusted nodes**. Structurally the entity-graph analogue of harmonic centrality (§4.15 doctrine); do not run separate link-metric plays for what is an entity-relationship problem.
* **The 4-Node Anchor Rule:** to make an entity association **permanent in model memory**, ground the claim across four connected dimensions:
  1. **Named Person Entity** (who made/holds the claim),
  2. **Known Physical Location** (verified GBP),
  3. **Exact Timestamp/Date**, and
  4. **Co-Present Verifiable Entities** (other recognized entities at the same event/claim).
* **Case (King of AEO — Dooley/Floate V6, distinct from Module 12's PR-manipulation episodes):** a crowning claim anchored to a specific date, a verified GBP location (Leigh Sports Village), and 5 recognized speaker entities became universally recognized across Claude, Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Google AI Mode. **20+ competitors failed to displace it using 15–20 Exact Match Domains** — EMDs lack multi-node graph relationships and lose to 4-node-anchored claims.
* **⚠️ Guardrail:** synthetic relationship claims must have a verifiable real-world footprint — every anchor node must independently check out (person real, GBP real, date real, co-present entities real). Fabricated anchors are the §12.4 failure mode with worse durability, because model memory holds them *permanently*.
