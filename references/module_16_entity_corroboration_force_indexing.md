# Module 16: Entity Corroboration & Discovery

*Source: James Dooley ("Money-Hat SEO") & TJ Robertson / The Edward Show, Episodes 1116 & 1134*

Before syndicating claims, finish on-page pruning in `kirby-aiseo-skill` Module 2 §2.34 so weak URLs are not corroborated.

To dominate Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and AI Overviews, you must actively "clarify" your entity across the highest-trust nodes on the web. AI search engines map the world via semantic triples (Subject → Predicate → Object) and look for multi-domain consensus. A single claim on a brand website is often ignored.

### **16.1 High-Trust Node Clarification**
*   **Target Nodes:** AI engines heavily weight canonical databases. You must inject your entity into Wikidata, Crunchbase, IMDb (if applicable), and industry-specific registries. SaaS **launch** directories (Product Hunt, BetaList, gap audits) are Module 18 §18.13 — this section remains canonical entity parity, not directory link-building.
*   **Data Parity:** Ensure the data across these nodes is mathematically identical (same address format, same executive names, same founding dates). Discrepancies cause LLM hallucination and citation dropping.
*   **Vertical Directory `About` Hard-Numbers Rule (Kasra Dash V4 Tier #5):** When populating the `About` or description fields on vertical trade, B2B, and local directories (Clutch, G2, Yelp, Checkatrade, Trustpilot, industry registers), lead with **verifiable hard numbers** — exact years in business, total verified jobs/deployments completed, active review counts, named certifications/registrations, and quantifiable response/delivery SLAs — rather than generic marketing prose. Ensure every metric matches the on-domain SSOT (`"My Business Facts"` / `/trust` hub) 1:1.

### **16.2 Cross-Platform Corroboration**
*   **The Consensus Playbook:** Take a single piece of high-value social proof (e.g., a major client testimonial or an industry award).
*   **Syndication:** Syndicate that *exact* claim across Reddit, LinkedIn, Twitter, and niche podcasts.
*   **The Result:** Because the claim is identical and hosted on multiple DA 90+ domains, it may lead AI search engines to treat it as corroborated (directional, single-source). This may modestly increase the likelihood it gets pulled into the AI Overview snippet as a trusted citation (directional, single-source).
*   **Pairing External Consensus (#3–#5) with the On-Domain `/trust` Hub (#1) (Kasra Dash V4):** In the 8-tier AI SEO trust hierarchy, third-party consensus nodes (Tier #3 review platforms, Tier #4 editorial roundups, Tier #5 vertical directories) drive **unbranded discovery**, while the on-domain **Self-Corroboration Hub (`/trust` — *"Can you trust [Brand]?"*, Tier #1 in [`kirby-aiseo-skill`](../../kirby-aiseo-skill/SKILL.md) Module 2 §2.38/§2.51)** resolves **branded verification and risk checks** (dated operational numbers, vetting/QC steps, explicit guarantees with honest limits, plain-text third-party review consensus with outbound links and **NO `AggregateRating` JSON-LD**, plus skeptical FAQs). Always pair off-page corroboration (#3–#5) with the on-domain `/trust` hub (#1) so retrieval engines verify identical hard numbers on both sides of the citation graph.

### **16.5 The Multi-Node Anchor Framework & Entity Juice (V6-D)**

*Source: James Dooley / Charles Floate — "Entity SEO for AI Search" (V6), 2026. Single-source, directional.*

* **Entity Juice (metric reframing):** replaces link-juice thinking for entity SEO — an entity's strength is its **confidence** (verified nodes), **relationship clarity** (unambiguous triples), and **proximity to trusted nodes**. Structurally the entity-graph analogue of harmonic centrality (§4.15 doctrine); do not run separate link-metric plays for what is an entity-relationship problem.
* **The 4-Node Anchor Rule:** to make an entity association more durable across retrieval systems (not guaranteed to be permanent in any model), ground the claim across four connected dimensions:
  1. **Named Person Entity** (who made/holds the claim),
  2. **Known Physical Location** (verified GBP),
  3. **Exact Timestamp/Date**, and
  4. **Co-Present Verifiable Entities** (other recognized entities at the same event/claim).
* **Case (King of AEO):** an invented term and a fabricated event; see Module 12 (§12.1, §12.4). Do not execute. Reported outcome claims are unverified.
* **⚠️ Guardrail:** any relationship claim must be true and independently verifiable; do not manufacture associations even with real anchors. Every anchor node must independently check out (person real, GBP real, date real, co-present entities real). Fabricated anchors are the §12.4 failure mode with worse durability than PR-based fabrications — once ingested into model memory they outlive the §12.6 decay cycle and resist correction.

### **16.6 48-Hour Opinion Sculpting & ORM at Scale (V6-H)**

* **The 10x Paradox (routing rule):** dominating a *head* term's generative answer is **10x harder** — a single query spawns 10–15 fan-outs and winning requires positive-sentiment consensus across all of them (ORM at scale: this module + Module 14 in [`kirby-aiseo-skill`](../../kirby-aiseo-skill/SKILL.md)). Capturing *long-tail* fan-out variants is **10x easier** — near-zero difficulty, exact-title + discovery plays (Module 4 §4.9) (documented as the scoped exception in `kirby-aiseo-skill` §2.45; do not duplicate the on-page SOP here).
* **48-Hour Opinion Sculpting:** high-level execution can fundamentally alter how LLMs describe and recommend a brand within ~48 hours by publishing **syndicated comparison assets** (Module 18 placements) plus **authoritative entity citations** (this module's nodes) — the same mechanism as Module 12's consensus economics, compressed. Directional, single-source (V6); a single-source exception, not the §4.8 window (see [`kirby-seo-timing-benchmarks`](../../kirby-seo-timing-benchmarks/SKILL.md): 2–4 weeks for AIO/Gemini, 60–120 days for ChatGPT Search).
* **Cadence guardrail:** 48-hour sculpting rides the §12.6 4–6-week decay cliff — sculpted consensus without maintenance decays; schedule reload cadence for any sculpted claim.
