## When to Use
- You need to build multi-platform entity consensus.
- You are vetting paid newswire/press placements for guaranteed indexation, defending against fraudulent competitor DMCA takedowns (pre-position, counter-notice, §512(f) escalation), or auditing host platforms for self-referential canonical traps (Module 21).
- You are optimizing YouTube transcripts or executing Parasite SEO.
- You are planning backlink acquisition or PR syndication to manipulate AI Overviews (AEO press releases live here in Module 12, not in `kirby-technical-aeo`).
- You are structuring front-loaded PR Newswire headlines to rank directly on Page 1 for commercial queries (Module 12 §12.8), or auditing media buys to enforce the publisher subfolder exclusion rule (§12.9).
- You are defending against negative AI Overviews by establishing and moderating a branded subreddit, or navigating the 2026 administrative anti-spam crackdown and ambassador protocol (Module 20 §20.4).
- You are transcribing influencer YouTube review videos on your primary domain with FTC Part 465 disclosures to seed LLM consensus (Module 4 §4.23).
- You are building owned assets to intercept "[Keyword] Reddit" modifier searches.
- You are using micro-influencers and short-form video for indexable social proof.
- You are anchoring entity consensus via Wikidata, Crunchbase, or cross-platform social corroboration (on-page pruning first: `kirby-aiseo-skill` Module 2 §2.34).
- You are placing a SaaS/B2B brand into third-party listicles/roundups, running competitive link-gap outreach, or pricing editorial insertions (Module 18). First-party “Best X” pages stay in `kirby-aiseo-skill` Module 2 §2.16. Before initiating off-page outreach, check SERP undertargeting (`kirby-aiseo-skill` §2.29); defer link building if on-page targeting can rank first (§18.1.1). LinkedIn parasite *posts* stay in Module 4 §4.14; LinkedIn *DMs for placements* are Module 18.
- You are evaluating which asset should hold a commercial head term, whether a high-authority social post can beat exact-match domains and link-built networks, or how a "Positive Ranking State" expands query radius beyond the written page (Module 19). LinkedIn production mechanics stay in Module 4 §4.14; Module 19 holds the ranking-substrate doctrine, including per-market/geo-fenced top social results.
- You are planning PR syndication budgets and must choose between a one-off release and a reload schedule: press releases decay after 4–6 weeks, so durable commercial terms need recurring syndication every 4–6 weeks or permanent tier-2 links into the PR URLs, while transient/event queries can run on a single PR (Module 12 §12.6). For evergreen terms prefer owned assets + entity corroboration (`kirby-aiseo-skill` §2.35 / Module 16).
- You are screening link targets by harmonic centrality / seed-graph distance or acquiring DR 10–20 high-harmonic domains (Module 4 §4.15 — self-contained twin of `kirby-aiseo-skill` §1.17).
- You are executing the 90-day branded-window guest-post perimeter for brand-name positions 1–30 (Module 4 §4.16) or direct cash local link outreach at $100–$500/post (Module 4 §4.17 — disclosure guardrail applies).
- You are placing paid advertorials (Module 12 §12.7 — static raw-HTML placement conditions + disclosure guardrail).
- You are seeding directory reviews on the zero-review void (Module 18 §18.21 — genuine sourcing only) or deciding whether to disavow (Module 18 §18.22 — never prophylactically; digital PR +71% benchmark).
- You are composing, sequencing, or scheduling ANY outbound outreach email — link-gap, listicle placement, PR pitching, guest-post, or cash local outreach: enforce the empirical reply-rate mandates (60–90 word body, 1–2 follow-up cap, sub-30-character subject line, Tuesday/Wednesday dispatch, value-first open, editors-not-CEOs targeting — Module 22).
- You are working the weighted source hierarchy (Google Books/YouTube/Wikipedia), IMDb Pro profiles, Google ecosystem stacking (sites.google.com, NotebookLM, Events), podcast `Event` entities, 4-node entity anchoring, or 48-hour opinion sculpting (Module 16 §16.3–§16.6).

- You are acquiring nofollow backlinks or evaluating whether nofollow links pass ranking authority: apply Kyle Roof's Two-Factor Law (host page ranks + gets clicks), the May 2024 Google API clickstream data, and the Semrush 2025 AI visibility correlation (Module 23).
- You are engineering AI search engine and LLM consensus to name an individual or brand as the top expert in a niche: deploy the Pedro Diaz LinkedIn Index-0 formula, tiered Yahoo Finance wire syndication (Zean Yasin case study), David Quaid on-site entity proclamations, and balanced self-promotional listicles (Module 24).
- You are filtering raw competitor backlink CSV exports via LLM prompts to strip out 95% of scraper/directory spam and classify prospects into the 4 high-yield buckets (Vendor/Partner Directories, Regional Chambers, Resource Insertions, and Editorial Roundups) paired with Module 22 outreach constraints (Module 25).
- You are building an on-site **"Reddit Alternative Resource Hub"** (synthesizing multi-thread community complaints and pricing into structured comparison tables that rank alongside or above Reddit) or executing the LinkedIn Entity Hashtag + NotebookLM Audio Overview loop to capture Google AI Overviews (Module 26; distinct from basic title interception in M13 and branded subreddit defense in M20).
- You are optimizing off-page review and directory signals for **ChatGPT local recommendations**: execute the **Yelp Data Licensing Mandate** (OpenAI pulls local star ratings, photos, and Request-a-Quote cards directly from Yelp) alongside multi-platform consensus across Trustpilot, Clutch, and regional chambers (Module 27; Yelp solicitation guardrails in `../../kirby-local-seo/SKILL.md` Module 6 §6.12).
- You are engineering high-authority Digital PR link magnets from proprietary customer datasets, deploying client-side interactive calculators (e.g., sustainability/savings tools), evaluating disavow penalty recovery boundaries, or leveraging ranking nofollow links to trigger dual-surface SERP video co-ranking (Module 28).
