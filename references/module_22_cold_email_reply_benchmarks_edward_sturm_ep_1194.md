# Module 22: Cold-Email Reply-Rate Benchmarks — A Reported 6,337-Email Dataset

*Source: Edward Sturm, The Edward Show, Episode E1194, "6,337 Cold Emails Reveal What Actually Gets Replies (And Backlinks)" (13:01) — reported study (single source, unverified) across Reddit link-building and PR outreach. October 2026.*

These are reported, single-source, unverified benchmarks; treat them as starting hypotheses to test, not rules. Use them as defaults for outbound email this skill generates: link-gap outreach (Module 18), PR syndication pitching (Module 12), guest-post perimeter and cash local outreach (Module 4 §4.16–§4.17).

**Consent Rule (all outreach email):** identify the sender accurately in every message, include a working unsubscribe/opt-out and honour opt-outs promptly, and confirm a lawful basis for the recipient's market before sending (e.g., US CAN-SPAM, UK PECR/UK GDPR).

### **22.1 The Word-Count Reply Curve**

| Email length | Reported reply rate | Drop-off |
|---|---|---|
| Under 100 words | **11.9%** | — |
| 100–200 words | 1.9% | −84% |
| Over 200 words | 0.3% | −97.5% |

**Suggested starting length: 60–90 words.** The reported cliff between <100 and 100–200 is the single largest controllable factor in the dataset: crossing it destroys roughly five-sixths of reply probability. Draft outreach at 60–90 words and treat any edit that pushes past ~100 words as a likely regression until your own data says otherwise.

### **22.2 Follow-Up Sequence Math**

| Sequence | Reported reply rate | Lift |
|---|---|---|
| Initial email alone | 4.1% | — |
| +1 follow-up | **6.6%** | +61% |
| +2 follow-ups | 6.9% | marginal |

**Reported plateau after 2 follow-ups.** The first follow-up is where nearly all the lift lives; the second is nearly free; the third and beyond yield diminishing returns while risking domain reputation and spam-folder burn — which taxes every future email from the domain. **Cap sequences at 1–2 follow-ups.**

### **22.3 Subject Line Architecture**

- **Under 30 characters: 83.7% reported open rate**, vs 69.5% for subject lines over 30 characters (no sample split or sender-reputation control reported; open tracking is unreliable).
- **Numbers in subject lines reportedly lift open rates.**

### **22.4 Send Timing**

**Tuesday and Wednesday reportedly outperform Thursday and Friday** for B2B and link outreach (no sample or sender-reputation caveat reported). Prefer Tue/Wed slots and test against your own send data.

### **22.5 Value-First Pitching Protocols**

**Never lead with an ask.** Lead with immediate, verifiable value:

- Point out a **broken link** on their page.
- Offer a **fresh original statistic** they cannot get elsewhere.
- Submit a **technical correction** (outdated figure, mislabeled chart, factual error).

The ask (link, placement, coverage) arrives only after the value has landed.

### **22.6 Recipient Targeting**

Pitch **department managers and content editors** — the people who own the page and can say yes unilaterally. CEOs and generic inboxes (`info@`, `contact@`) route to deletion. Identify the page owner before composing; if the owner cannot be identified, the target is not ready for outreach.
