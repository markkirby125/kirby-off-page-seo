# Module 22: Cold-Email Reply-Rate Benchmarks — The 6,337-Email Empirical Dataset

*Source: Edward Sturm, The Edward Show, Episode E1194, "6,337 Cold Emails Reveal What Actually Gets Replies (And Backlinks)" (13:01) — empirical study across Reddit link-building and PR outreach. October 2026.*

These are measured reply-rate curves, not heuristics. They govern every outbound email this skill generates: link-gap outreach (Module 18), PR syndication pitching (Module 12), guest-post perimeter and cash local outreach (Module 4 §4.16–§4.17).

### **22.1 The Word-Count Reply Curve**

| Email length | Reply rate | Drop-off |
|---|---|---|
| Under 100 words | **11.9%** | — |
| 100–200 words | 1.9% | −84% |
| Over 200 words | 0.3% | −97.5% |

**The optimal length is 60–90 words.** The cliff between <100 and 100–200 is the single largest controllable factor in the dataset: crossing it destroys roughly five-sixths of reply probability. Draft outreach at 60–90 words and treat any edit that pushes past ~100 words as a regression.

### **22.2 Follow-Up Sequence Math**

| Sequence | Reply rate | Lift |
|---|---|---|
| Initial email alone | 4.1% | — |
| +1 follow-up | **6.6%** | +61% |
| +2 follow-ups | 6.9% | marginal |

**Hard plateau after 2 follow-ups.** The first follow-up is where nearly all the lift lives; the second is nearly free; the third and beyond yield diminishing returns while risking domain reputation and spam-folder burn — which taxes every future email from the domain. **Cap sequences at 1–2 follow-ups.**

### **22.3 Subject Line Architecture**

- **Under 30 characters: 83.7% open rate**, vs 69.5% for subject lines over 30 characters — a 14-point open-rate premium for brevity alone.
- **Numbers in subject lines significantly lift open rates.**

### **22.4 Send Timing**

**Tuesday and Wednesday significantly outperform Thursday and Friday** for B2B and link-outreach deliverability. Schedule dispatch accordingly; never send a campaign spine on Thu/Fri when a Tue/Wed slot is available.

### **22.5 Value-First Pitching Protocols**

**Never lead with an ask.** Lead with immediate, verifiable value:

- Point out a **broken link** on their page.
- Offer a **fresh original statistic** they cannot get elsewhere.
- Submit a **technical correction** (outdated figure, mislabeled chart, factual error).

The ask (link, placement, coverage) arrives only after the value has landed.

### **22.6 Recipient Targeting**

Pitch **department managers and content editors** — the people who own the page and can say yes unilaterally. CEOs and generic inboxes (`info@`, `contact@`) route to deletion. Identify the page owner before composing; if the owner cannot be identified, the target is not ready for outreach.
