# Module 15: The Micro-Influencer Indexation Play

*Source: Edward Sturm / The Edward Show, Episode 1130*

### **15.1 The Shift from Press Releases to Micro-Creators**
Traditional off-page PR syndication (e.g., $80 wire press releases) has heavily degraded in efficacy as Google and AI search engines filter out automated syndication networks. The modern replacement is leveraging tiny "micro-influencers" (creators with only a few hundred to a few thousand followers) across Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube Shorts.

### **15.2 Indexability vs. Audience Size**
The entire strategy relies on **Indexation**, not audience reach. Follower count is completely irrelevant. 
Before paying a creator, validate their back catalogue: paste the descriptions of their older videos verbatim into Google to confirm that their content gets indexed and ranked.

### **15.3 Execution: The $25–$50 Publishing Engine**
1.  **Pitch & Price:** Approach micro-creators offering $25–$50 per post around a high-intent, overlooked keyword that competitors ignore (e.g., "residential general contractor software").
2.  **The Keyword-Loaded Payload:** Supply the creator with a pre-written, keyword-dense description (utilizing the ~2,000-character limit on Instagram). Place the exact target keyword in the very first sentence.
3.  **Cross-Platform Syndication:** Have the creator post the video and description across Instagram Reels, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.
4.  **Cluster with First-Party Content:** After 3–5 creators have posted, publish your own supporting first-party landing page with the identical target keyword in the page title, URL slug, H1, and opening sentence.

### **15.4 The AI Social Proof Mechanism**
Why this works: Short-form video platforms index within hours and persist in search results for years. More importantly, as platforms like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity increasingly treat social networks as live search engines, these keyword-loaded descriptions get cited as third-party "social proof" or entity corroboration for your brand, drastically undercutting traditional link-building costs.
